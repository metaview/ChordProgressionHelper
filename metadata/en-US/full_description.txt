A music app to write chord progressions, add simple drum beats and a little
melody — to play, sing and perform along with your phone.

Build a song from parts. Each part is made of measures, and for every measure
you choose the chords and how they sound.

Features:
- Chords: pick from diatonic (step), dominant seventh and parallel chords
- Strumming: set a strumming pattern per measure
- Drums: add a simple drum beat to keep the groove
- Melody: add a little melody line per measure
- Play back your progression to sing and perform along
- Compose a song made of progressions

No account, no ads, no tracking.
